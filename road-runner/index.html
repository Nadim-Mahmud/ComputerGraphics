Speed: 0.5

Road Runner controls

Drive the car · Wireframe

A / D
Steer left / right. The car stays within the road edges.
W / S
Increase / decrease speed. Speed stays between 0.5 and the configured maximum.

The navigation bar shows your current speed.

Move the camera · Cube and Wireframe

↑ / ↓
Move the camera forward / backward.
← / →
Move the camera left / right.

Adjust camera position using the arrow keys.
