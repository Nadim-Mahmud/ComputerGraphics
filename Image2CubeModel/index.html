Saba Island Landscape

Dev: Nadim Mahmud

Controls

Level 0: ← / → move left and right, ↑ / ↓ move forward and backward.

  • Levels 1–4: ← / → orbit around the landscape.
  • Move closer / farther: ↑ / ↓ (camera height stays the same).
  • Zoom: the + / − buttons (bottom right) or the mouse wheel.
  • Brightness: keyboard + / - (levels 1–4).
  • Levels: 0 cube, 1 random landscape, 2 island from images, 3 island with clouds, 4 island with clouds and rippling sea.
